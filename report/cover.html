FlowerNet:

A custom dataset of 300 flower images — daisy, rose and sunflower, 100 images per class — is classified with five deep learning models under 5-fold stratified cross-validation. The project applies data augmentation, compares five complementary evaluation metrics, and visualises learning curves to detect overfitting.
